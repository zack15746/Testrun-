Elemental Bender VR

Setup: put your controllers down. In Quest settings turn on Hand Tracking, then open this page in the Quest Browser.

Pinch thumb + index finger: switch element (Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Lightning).
Push your palm forward fast: throw Fire, Water, Air or a Lightning bolt that chains between enemies.
Earth: slam a hand down to raise a rock, then push forward to launch it. Hold your palm steady to spray pebbles.
Charge attack: make a fist and hold it still, above your waist, for about a second. The glow on your hand grows. Then open your hand or push to release a huge attack (Fire: inferno ball, Water: ice lance, Earth: boulder, Air: tornado, Lightning: storm). It costs chi while charging.
Every element has 3 moves: PUSH forward = shot. HOLD your palm steady = stream (Fire: flames, Water: whip, Earth: pebble spray, Air: wind that shoves enemies back, Lightning: an auto-aimed arc beam). SLAM a hand down = Fire: flame burst, Water: tidal wave that also heals you, Air: cyclone that clears bolts, Lightning: thunder strike (Earth: raises a rock).
Hold your open palm up and steady to breathe a continuous Fire stream or Water whip. Sweep your hand to whip the water around. Streams take a moment to burn through a target.
Your elements evolve: every 3 kills with an element levels it up (max 8), refills your chi and raises your max chi. Attacks get bigger and stronger, and from level 3 your hits explode and take out nearby enemies. Enemies fire slow purple bolts at you. Your chi (blue bar) drains as you bend and refills when you rest, so pace yourself. Turn your sound on!
Block: cross your arms by holding both hands together in front of your chest to raise a shield (it drains chi). Bolts hit the shield instead of you.
Clashes: when your attack meets an enemy bolt, the stronger one wins and the weaker one is cancelled. The winner loses some power. Higher levels hit harder, and big enemy bolts hit harder too.
Every 5th wave has a boss!
Clap your hands together hard for a big shockwave (30 chi). It damages and pushes back every enemy around you and wipes out incoming bolts.
Dodge by moving your head, or shoot them down. Clear a wave to heal a bit!
