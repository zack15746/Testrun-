Civ VR – v1

Start alone. Mine trees and rocks. Explore an endless world.

Quest: left stick moves, right stick snap-turns, swing the tool in your right hand at trees and rocks (right stick click switches tool). X/Y choose a recipe, A crafts, B toggles build mode (trigger places, grip removes). Map, counts and recipes are on your left wrist.

Desktop preview: click to look, WASD to move, click to swing, T switches tool, C chooses a recipe, E crafts, B build mode (click places, X removes).
