Shinobi Trials

Four areas to conquer: beat each area boss (every 5th wave) to travel on. Level up to evolve your jutsu. Leap over the red dash line and sidestep fireballs. Survive waves of rogue ninjas.

Trigger: throw kunai · Grip (hold, release): charge Spirit Orb · Left stick: move · Right stick: turn · A: shadow dash · B: leap · Left X or clap: Shadow Clones · Left Y: Storm Palm · Both hands above your head: Mind Veil · Both hands beside your face: Spirit Eye · Touch an enemy with a charged orb for a Spiral Orb strike
