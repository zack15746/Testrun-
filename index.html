⚔️ Giant Hunters VR

Swing between rooftops, dodge the giants and slice the glowing blue crystal on the back of each neck.

  • Trigger (either hand): fire grapple hook, hold to reel in
  • Left stick: walk on ground or rooftops
  • Swing a blade fast through the glowing blue crystal on the neck to kill
  • Reeling uses gas; it refills when you stand on a surface
  • A green dot shows what your grapple can hit
  • From wave 3, some giants run
  • Giants turn slowly: circle around behind them for the nape
  • Slice arms off so they cannot hit you, or legs off so they slow down or drop low (limbs regrow after about 10 seconds)
