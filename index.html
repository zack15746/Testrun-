Elemental Dungeon VR

Hand-tracking only • Unique maps • No weapons
Setup: Put controllers down. Enable Hand Tracking in Quest settings, then open this page in the Quest Browser.

Combat (hands only):
• Pinch thumb + index → cycle element (Fire → Water → Earth → Air → Lightning)
• Push palm forward fast → throw shot
• Hold open palm steady → continuous stream / whip / spray / arc beam
• Slam hand down → area special (burst / tidal wave+heal / rock raise / cyclone / thunder)
• Earth: slam raises a rock; push it to launch
• Charge: make a fist and hold still above waist ~1s, then open/push for a huge attack
• Cross both hands in front of chest → shield (drains chi)
• Clap hard → shockwave (costs 30 chi)

Dungeon: Clear waves in each zone to open the portal. Step into the glowing portal to travel to the next unique map. Elements level up every 3 kills and grow stronger. Rest to recover chi. Dodge with your body.
